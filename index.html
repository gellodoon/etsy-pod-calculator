Understanding Etsy Fees (2026)

Etsy charges several fees that are automatically deducted from your sales before the money hits your bank account:

  • Listing Fee: A flat $0.20 per item listed or renewed.
  • Transaction Fee: Etsy takes 6.5% of the total order amount (including shipping and gift wrap).
  • Payment Processing Fee: For US sellers, this is typically 3% + $0.25 per transaction (rates vary by country).

Why Margins Matter in Print on Demand

Because you are paying a third-party manufacturer to print and ship your items, your base costs are higher than handmade sellers. Maintaining at least a 20-30% profit margin ensures you have enough budget left over for Etsy Ads and promotional discounts.
